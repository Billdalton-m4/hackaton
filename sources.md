# Research sources to verify and cite

Use primary or authoritative sources for any factual claims in the final video or post. Open the original reports and verify the date, reporting period, and exact wording before quoting a number.

1. **Communications Authority of Kenya — Cybersecurity reports**  
   https://www.ca.go.ke/  
   Look for the National KE-CIRT/CC cybersecurity report relevant to the reporting period you discuss. Do not quote a statistic until you have confirmed it in the report itself.

2. **Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) — Secure Our World**  
   https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world  
   General guidance on strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, software updates, and recognizing phishing.

3. **National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) — Cybersecurity resources**  
   https://www.nist.gov/cybersecurity  
   Technical and educational resources for cybersecurity practices.

4. **Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) — Internet of Things resources**  
   https://www.cisa.gov/  
   Search the official CISA site for current IoT security guidance and cite the specific page used.

## Citation checklist

- Include the source name and report/page title in the video description or end card.
- For statistics, include the reporting period and publication date.
- Distinguish a real documented incident from an illustrative stock or AI-generated visual.
- Do not imply AI-generated imagery depicts an actual Kenyan incident.
