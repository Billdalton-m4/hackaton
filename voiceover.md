# Voiceover and scene plan

**Working title:** One Digital World, Many Cyber Risks  
**Topic:** Cloud, IoT & Critical Infrastructure  
**Target duration:** 55–60 seconds  
**Format:** Vertical video (9:16)

## Voiceover draft

Every day, we depend on technology — from cloud storage and smart devices to the systems that support hospitals, businesses, and electricity networks.

But how safe are these systems?

Weak passwords, outdated software, and poorly secured devices can create opportunities for cybercriminals to steal information or disrupt essential services.

In Kenya, as our digital world grows, protecting these systems becomes increasingly important.

So, what can we do? Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, update your devices, and secure your online accounts.

Cybersecurity is not just the job of experts. It is everyone's responsibility.

Secure your devices. Protect your data. Help build a safer digital Kenya.

## Recording tips

- Record in a quiet room and keep the phone about a hand-span from your mouth.
- Speak clearly at a steady pace; leave short pauses between sections.
- Lower background music so every word is easy to hear.
- Keep captions readable and inside the phone-safe area.
